Snooker by Gjendy launches in January 2027. This is where to reach us, before and after.

Contact

support@gjendy.com

We reply in English or Norwegian, usually within a few days.

Helpful details

When you write about a problem, it helps to include:

  • Your phone model and its iOS or Android version
  • The game version (shown in the app menu)
  • What you were doing, and what you expected to happen

Quick answers

Is the game free?

Yes. Version 1.0 is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game works fully offline.

Can I turn the aiming help off?

Yes. Choose Beginner, Normal or Pro, or set each help option on its own under Custom. Pro shows no help on screen. The rules are always the full professional rules, except that the miss rule is off on Beginner.

I am left-handed.

You can move the cue to the other side in the match settings.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Your records and progress are stored only on your phone, since we collect no data. They follow you to a new phone only if your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) restores the app data.

Your data

The game does not collect personal data, so there is nothing for us to delete or export. Read the privacy policy.
